## PCB options

- 6 layers, 95 x 60 mm, 1.6 mm, outline with 3 mm corner radius and four 3.0 mm holes.
- Layer order: F.Cu, In1 (GND plane), In2, In3, In4 (power islands + GND fill), B.Cu (GND pour).
- Stackup: choose thin dielectric next to the planes and a thick core between In2 and In3, for example
  0.1 / 0.2 / 0.8 / 0.2 / 0.1 mm (see the manual, "Signal integrity"). No impedance control is needed.
- Design minimums: 0.10 mm track and clearance, 0.4 / 0.2 mm vias, 0.3 mm copper to edge.
- Surface finish: ENIG recommended (flat pads for the 0.5 mm-pitch LFCSP DACs).

## Assembly

- **Global Sourcing** (zero JLCPCB stock on 2 Nov 2026): U1, U2 AD3552RBCPZ16; U3-U6, U16-U19 ADA4807-1ARJZ-R7;
  J1, J12 Samtec ESW-113-33-T-D.
- **Through-hole**: J2 barrel jack, J4-J11 SMA (top); J1, J12 elevated sockets (**bottom side**). Order through-hole
  assembly or fit these by hand. J1/J12 (Samtec ESW) are rated for wave or hand soldering only, not lead-free reflow.
- Not placed: TP1 (bare test pad), H1-H4 (mounting holes).
- **Check rotations in JLCPCB's placement preview** before confirming. Their library footprints are not always
  rotated like KiCad's; the usual suspects are SOT-23 (D1-D8, U3-U6, U14-U19), TSSOP-16 (U8-U11), SOIC-8 (U7),
  MSOP/HVSSOP (U12, U13), the LFCSP DACs (pin 1 marked on the silkscreen) and the electrolytic C57 (polarity).
- Orientation is mixed within some repeated groups: U4 and U6 sit at 270 deg while U3 and U5 are at 0 deg, U11 at
  180 deg while U8-U10 are at 90 deg, and the BAV99 clamps D1-D4 / D5 / D6-D8 at 90 / 180 / 270 deg. This is
  intentional (set by the routing); give these parts a second look in the placement preview.
- U1/U2 use the project footprint `ad3552r_custom:LFCSP-32-1EP_5x5mm_P0.5mm_EP3.6x3.6mm_AD3552R`: the exposed-pad
  land matches the AD3552R's 3.6 mm pad (CP-32-30).
- F1 is the Polytronics SMD1206P050TF/24 (0.5 A hold, **24 V**). The Littelfuse 1206L050YR first chosen is rated
  only 6 V, too low for the 12 V input.
- Substitutions against the original design: L1 Sunlord SWPA5040S3R3NT (Taiyo NR5040 out of stock; pads checked
  against the NR-50xx footprint, fits); R66 130 k instead of 131 k (+2V5 = 2.48 V).
- Re-check stock on the day with `python3 tools/jlc_check.py`; if the Nexperia 74AVC4T774PWJ is short, the TI
  SN74AVC4T774PWR (C129363) is the same part.
